Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): a program that writes two lines — a warning to one's past self about one specific moment, and one sentence on why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

## The entry

> Don't lend your motorbike to Doni on graduation night — he swore he'd bring it back by ten.
> He didn't, and you spent the next three years walking to every job interview that bike would have carried you to.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt (205 characters, 2 lines)
```

Requires Node 22.18+ (TypeScript runs as-is).
